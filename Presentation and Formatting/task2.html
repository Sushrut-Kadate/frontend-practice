India Moves Towards a Greener Future

The Government recently stated that renewable energy is not an option but necessity for sustainable growth.

Why Renewable Energy ?

India is expanding its capacity for solar and wind projects. Experts believe this shift will create opportunities for millions. The Green Energy Mission has already gained international recognition.

For more than a decade , researchers have warned about the consequences of fossile fuel consumption. Studies show that adopting renewable enrgy can significantly reduce the pollution and stabalize the climate system.

Global Response

Countries across the world are investing heavily in clean technology.According to recent study ,the green energy sector is expected to grow by 40% in the next five years.
